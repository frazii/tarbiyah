<Annotation id="an-landing" targetId="ab-landing" placement="bottom">
White ground, green primary, gold reserved for Arabic calligraphy accents. The prayer strip is the same utility widget used inside the app — next prayer big, the rest as chips.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-login" targetId="ab-login" placement="bottom">
Every center gets /login?center=slug: their logo, name and brand color on the sign-in card. Brand colors are guarded at 4.5:1 contrast so the button text always reads.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-parent-dash" targetId="ab-parent-dash" placement="bottom">
Parents land on their children: latest attendance badge, outstanding invoices with Pay now, and permission slips due. Accounts are admin-invited only.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-child" targetId="ab-child" placement="bottom">
School Bytes parity: a month calendar of attendance with a gold ring on today and a gold dot on reported absences. The absence form feeds the teacher's roll directly.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-attendance" targetId="ab-attendance" placement="bottom">
The roll is one row per student with filled status chips. A parent's absence notice pre-selects Excused and prefixes the note with the reason — the teacher just confirms.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-fees" targetId="ab-fees" placement="bottom">
One table for both sides: parents pay online (demo — Stripe is the roadmap item), admins record cash. Paid rows keep their method for the audit trail.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-admin" targetId="ab-admin" placement="bottom">
Center admins manage only their center: accounts, branding, sign-in link. Tenant scoping (centerScope) is enforced in every query, not just the UI.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-portal" targetId="ab-portal" placement="bottom">
Super admin only. Onboarding creates the center and its first admin in one step; the report table color-codes 30-day attendance and exports as CSV.
</Annotation>

<Annotation id="an-mobile" targetId="ab-mobile" placement="bottom">
The whole app is responsive — same routes, drawer nav under 1024px. The prayer widget wraps to stacked rows; its sky follows the local time of day.
</Annotation>
